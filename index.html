About

Brand positioning, wired to customer acquisition.

I've spent my career between entrepreneurial ventures and larger technology companies. That mix taught me how to connect the way a brand positions itself with the way it actually wins customers.

I led organic growth at WalkMe and AI21 Labs, and today I run it at ZyG. The question I keep coming back to: what does it take to build a brand when AI agents are the ones discovering, comparing and recommending?

I write about what I learn, including what worked, what failed, and what other founders and business leaders can take and apply.

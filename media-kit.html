Long

Rotem Gal is a Tel Aviv-based marketer and writer focused on branding, organic growth and how brands get chosen in an agentic world. Rotem is Director of Organic Growth at ZyG and previously worked at AI21 Labs and WalkMe. Rotem's writing on marketing, ecommerce, branding and customer behavior has appeared in Inc., Entrepreneur, business.com and ReferralCandy, and Rotem is a speaker at Becoming 10x, the annual summit by 10x Marketers, in Tel Aviv in November 2026. Across entrepreneurial ventures and larger technology companies, Rotem's work has centred on one question: how companies become known, trusted and chosen. At ZyG, which builds an agentic operating system for ecommerce brands, Rotem leads organic growth and studies how AI assistants and agents change the way consumers discover and evaluate brands. Rotem has appeared on the Voices of Search podcast and spoken at Team8 about organic growth for B2B companies.
